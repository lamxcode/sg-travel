Singapore Photography & Transit Protocol

Base: 47 Hongkong St Transit: Contactless / DT / NE Lines Currency: AUD / HKD
Day 2: Civic Core, Heritage Grid & Marina Bay Tue, Sep 29
08:00 - 08:45 Base Prep

Wake Up & Quick Lobby Breakfast

Wake up, pack gear, grab coffee and toast down in the 47 Hongkong St lobby lounge.

08:45 - 09:50 Popular Spot

Fort Canning Tree Tunnel

Walk across Coleman Bridge / Canning Rise to the north-eastern stairs. Upward spiral staircase framing with overhead canopy. Expect 30-40 min queue.

10:00 - 10:30 Architecture

Old Hill Street Police Station

Wide-angle facade framing of the 927 rainbow shutters across the River Valley Rd junction.

10:45 - 12:15 Heritage Walk

Chinatown & Telok Ayer Conservation Corridor

Walk south via Pagoda St to Buddha Tooth Relic Temple (rooftop orchid garden & prayer wheel). Cut across to Thian Hock Kheng Temple and Telok Ayer shophouse facades.

12:15 - 13:15 Hawker Lunch

Lunch: Amoy or Maxwell Food Centre

Han Kee Fish Soup / A Noodle Story at Amoy, or Tian Tian / Ah Tai at Maxwell (~SGD $8.00 | ~AUD $9.30 / HKD $47.50, Cash).

13:15 - 14:10 AC Buffer

Air-Conditioned Coffee Break

Hydrate and cool down inside a cafe near Telok Ayer or the ground lobby of CapitaSpring.

14:10 - 15:15 Lobby Queue

CapitaSpring L51 Sky Garden

Queue in the ground floor lobby by 14:10 for the 14:30 afternoon public opening. 360° open-air CBD panoramas (Free).

15:45 - 18:30 Conservatories

Gardens by the Bay: Cloud Forest & Flower Dome

Indoor mist waterfall and floral arrangements. Keep camera body zipped inside bag for 5 minutes after exiting to prevent lens fogging.

18:30 - 20:15 Light Show

Supertree Grove & Garden Rhapsody

Blue-hour wide-angle framing; watch the 19:45 light show directly beneath the canopy without rushing.

20:30 onwards Dinner

Dinner @ Marina Bay & Return

Dinner at Satay by the Bay or The Shoppes at MBS; stroll or take Downtown Line back to base.

Day 3: Art Deco Morning, Ridges & Dusk Skybridge Wed, Sep 30
08:15 - 08:45 Base Prep

Quick Coffee & Lobby Refreshment

Light toast and coffee in the lobby before boarding bus/train west.

09:15 - 11:30 Street Heritage

Tiong Bahru Art Deco Estate & Market

1930s SIT Streamline Moderne curves and spiral stairwells. Optional second breakfast at Tiong Bahru Market L2 (Jian Bo Shui Kueh ~SGD $3.20 | ~AUD $3.70 / HKD $19.00).

11:45 - 13:00 Lunch

Transit & Indoor Lunch @ VivoCity

Direct bus or train down to HarbourFront; air-conditioned lunch before coastal walk.

13:30 - 16:30 Canopy Walk

Sentosa & Fort Siloso Skywalk

Sentosa Express / Boardwalk to Fort Siloso. Elevated treetop walk with coastal views.

16:45 - 18:15 Golden Hour

Henderson Waves & Mount Faber

Bus/taxi directly up to Henderson Waves. Sculptural curved timber ribs illuminated by angled late afternoon light (no midday overhead glare).

18:45 - 20:30 Strict 21:00

The Pinnacle@Duxton Skybridge

Blk 1G Level 1 turnstile (SGD $6.00 contactless | ~AUD $7.00 / HKD $35.60). 50th-floor skyline twilight and night panorama well before admission cut-off.

20:45 onwards Dinner

Dinner: Duxton Hill / Keong Saik

Relaxed dinner in the restored shophouse dining enclave right below the Pinnacle towers.

Day 4: Flora Macro, Hawker Lunch & Departure Thu, Oct 01
08:00 - 08:30 Checkout

Hotel Checkout & Bag Storage

Complete checkout at 47 Hongkong St reception, store both 29" suitcases with the desk. Carry only camera gear.

08:35 - 10:30 Macro Floral

National Orchid Garden (Botanic Gardens)

Downtown Line to Botanic Gardens (DT9). Stroll through park to National Orchid Garden (SGD $15.00 entry | ~AUD $17.50 / HKD $89.00).

10:35 - 11:25 Hawker Lunch

Lunch: Adam Road Food Centre

Selera Rasa Nasi Lemak right by the station exit (~SGD $6.50 | ~AUD $7.60 / HKD $38.60, Cash). Substantial hot meal ahead of flight.

11:30 - 12:15 Luggage Pick

Downtown Line Return & Bag Retrieval

Take Downtown Line straight back to Fort Canning; retrieve both 29" bags from reception storage.

12:20 - 13:00 Direct Transit

Grab / Taxi to Jewel Changi

Direct vehicle transfer (~SGD $25.00-$30.00 | ~AUD $29.00-$35.00 / HKD $148.00-$178.00). Avoids lugging two large bags through MRT line transfers.

13:00 - 17:00 Flight SQ898

Early Check-in & Rain Vortex Hands-Free

Drop suitcases immediately at Jewel L1 Early Check-in lounge (SIA counters open 24h to 3h before flight). Photograph HSBC Rain Vortex unburdened before airside departure.
